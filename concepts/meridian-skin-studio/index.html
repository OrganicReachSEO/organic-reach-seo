Frequently Asked Questions

Patient Care Guide
Will my consultation require any upfront payment or commitment? +
No. All initial 45-minute consultations at Meridian Skin Studio are completely complimentary with zero treatment obligation. We provide a written treatment roadmap and let you take all the time you need to decide.
How do you prevent the unnatural or 'overfilled' appearance? +
We use conservative dosing, micro-droplet technique, and high-cohesivity hyaluronic formulations matched to bone density rather than soft tissue superficial layers. If a treatment does not serve your facial harmony, we will openly advise against it.
What is the typical recovery or social downtime after injectables? +
Most patients resume everyday social activities immediately. Neuromodulators carry zero downtime; contouring treatments may involve mild localized swelling for 24 to 48 hours, which resolves fully before your two-week review.
