(06) — QUESTIONS

Common questions.

Do you ever generate or buy fake positive reviews?

No. We never manipulate or generate reviews — we only pursue removal of reviews that violate a platform's own stated policy.

How long does a removal usually take?

It varies by platform and violation type. Some removals process within days; others take longer as we escalate through platform review teams.

What happens to reviews that don't violate policy?

We leave them alone. Instead, we manage professional responses so genuine feedback — positive or critical — gets addressed openly.

Is this available as a one-time cleanup?

No. Reputation exposure is ongoing, so this is offered exclusively as a monthly retainer with continuous monitoring and action.
